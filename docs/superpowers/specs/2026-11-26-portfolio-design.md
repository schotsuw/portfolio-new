# Saran Chotsuwan — Portfolio Website Design

Date: 2026-11-26 · Status: draft for approval

## 1. Intent

Saran is a Software Engineering student at UNB (Fredericton, graduating May 2027) aiming for machine-learning engineering roles. He wants a new personal portfolio that replaces his old one. It should borrow the simple, modern bento-grid look of dousanmiao.com, a Google UX designer's portfolio, and be filled with his own projects and experience.

**Audience:** recruiters.
**Success:** within about a minute, a recruiter knows who Saran is and what his experience is, from a short, catchy headline and a scannable grid. They can also open his résumé or contact him in one click.

The reference blueprint is `portfolio_replication_plan.md`, which Saran supplied and Google Antigravity generated by crawling dousanmiao.com. This design keeps that blueprint's stack, visual system and interaction patterns, except where this document says otherwise. Section 9 corrects the blueprint's known bugs.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
- A bento-grid home page on desktop and a dedicated single-column flow on mobile, matching the blueprint's feel: light and dark modes, liquid-glass buttons and chips, smooth scrolling, subtle motion.
- Four project case studies that open as full-screen overlays, each with its own shareable URL and link preview.
- All content in typed data files, so Saran can update projects, status and copy without touching components.
- Free hosting on Vercel, deployed from a GitHub repo under `schotsuw`.

**Non-goals**
- No store, 1:1 coaching, eBook, video-feedback, booking or checkout features. The blueprint's Store/Coaching/Feedback modals, `formsubmit.co`, Gumroad and Google Calendar integrations are all dropped.
- No contact form (email copy plus links is enough), no blog, no CMS, no analytics in v1.
- No preloader splash.
- No custom cursor in v1. It's optional polish, left out for accessibility and simplicity. It could come back later behind the blueprint's guards.

**Anti-goals** (the site fails even if it technically works)
- **It reads as a clone of dousanmiao.com.** Never reuse his monogram, Lottie files, videos, images, copy, his Android-green brand accent, or his "HKG → LHR" relocation card. Layout and style are inspiration only.
- **It overclaims.** The Introhive MCP card must say Saran did *test automation for the MCP tools*, not that he built the MCP. No Introhive metrics or confidential details are published.
- **It looks unfinished.** No grey "Coming soon" boxes. Unfinished work shows as an "In progress" chip plus a real description and a designed placeholder visual.
- **It breaks on iPhone Safari**, ignores reduced motion, or is slow to load.

## 3. Constraints

- **Honesty:** every claim must match what Saran actually did. Unverified copy is flagged `draft: true` in the data so he can review it.
- **Introhive:** general descriptions only, with no numbers, customer names or internal screenshots. Public product facts come from introhive.com, e.g. the Introhive MCP page: an MCP server connecting a firm's relationship data to AI tools such as Copilot, Claude and GPT.
- **Cost:** free tier only (Vercel Hobby). A custom domain is optional and Saran's call.
- **Fonts and assets** must be licensed for reuse. Open Runde is SIL OFL, so self-hosting is fine, and Inter is the fallback.
- **Browsers:** current Chrome, Safari (macOS and iOS), Firefox and Edge.

## 4. Approaches considered

1. **React + Vite single-page app with overlay routing (chosen by Saran).** Closest to the reference: case studies open as overlays over the grid. Downsides are a shared link preview for every URL, near-empty HTML for crawlers, and custom routing. The fix is a post-build step that emits a per-route `index.html` with route-specific `<title>`, description and Open Graph tags (Section 7.3).
2. **Astro with React islands** (Claude's recommendation). Static HTML per page, Markdown case studies and minimal JavaScript, with case studies as real pages using view transitions. Not chosen: Saran preferred the SPA overlay model.
3. **Next.js.** Heavier framework, server features unused for a static portfolio. Rejected.

## 6. Content model

`src/content/` holds typed TS modules, and every user-facing string lives here.

- `site.ts`: name, headline, sub-headline, status chip text (e.g. "Open to new-grad ML / SWE roles · May 2027"), email, GitHub URL, LinkedIn URL, résumé path, location ("Fredericton, NB"), timezone (`America/Moncton`, Atlantic Time).
- `projects.ts`: an array of `Project` objects with `slug`, `title`, `tagline`, `status: 'shipped' | 'in-progress'`, `role`, `org`, `timeline`, `stack[]`, `team`, `cardSize: 'tall' | 'medium' | 'small'`, `media` (image/video/placeholder-diagram id), `links[]`, `sections[]` (typed blocks: `text`, `stats`, `list`, `quote`, `image`, `diagram`), `draft: boolean`, `og` (title, description, image).
- `experience.ts`: the four roles from 5.1 for the Experience card, the career ruler (ticks 2023–2027) and the About overlay. Each has a title, org, start/end, one-line summary and `draft`.
- **Status chip** (draft): "ML Engineer (part-time) @ Introhive · new-grad roles from May 2027".
- `stack.ts`: grouped skills shown in the stack card (e.g. Python, PyTorch/scikit-learn, LangChain/LangGraph, RAG, FastAPI/Django, React, PostgreSQL, AWS). Mark `draft` until Saran confirms.
- `personal.ts`: the personality card (powerlifting PRs, with a link-out to the PR card app idea later).

Copy for headlines and taglines is drafted by Claude, flagged `draft: true`, and listed in a `CONTENT_TODO.md` checklist at the repo root.

## 7. Architecture

### 7.1 Stack (from the blueprint, with corrections)
- Vite + React + TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS v4 via `@tailwindcss/vite`, with `@custom-variant dark (&:where(.dark, .dark *));`.
- `motion` (import from `motion/react`) for in-view and overlay transitions.
- `lenis` for smooth scroll on desktop only. Overlays carry `data-lenis-prevent`, and Lenis is stopped while an overlay is open.
- `lucide-react` icons, plus simple-icons SVGs for brand logos in the stack card.
- No Lottie in v1: Saran has no Lottie assets, and the monogram is an SVG animated with CSS/motion.

### 7.2 Routing
- A tiny custom router on the History API, pathname only, with no `#/slug` duplication.
- `/` shows home. `/projects/:slug` shows home with that case-study overlay open. `/about` shows home with the About overlay open. These three routes are shareable.
- Opening an overlay from the grid uses `pushState`, so Back closes it. Esc and the close button call `history.back()` when the overlay was pushed from home, and otherwise navigate to `/`.
- An unknown path renders home, and the build emits a `404.html` copy of home with `noindex`.
- A case study has Prev/Next buttons that cycle projects in `projects.ts` order using `replaceState`.

### 7.3 Per-route HTML for link previews
- A post-build Node script reads `dist/index.html` and `projects.ts`. For each project it writes `dist/projects/<slug>/index.html` with a route-specific `<title>`, meta description, canonical URL, `og:*` and `twitter:*` tags. It does the same for `/about`.
- OG images are static 1200×630 PNGs per project in `public/og/`. Placeholders are generated from a branded template (name + project title on the site background) until real ones exist.
- `vercel.json` adds a rewrite of unmatched non-asset paths to `/index.html` as a fallback.
- Home `index.html` includes JSON-LD for `Person` and `ProfilePage` only. No `Product`/`Service`.

### 7.4 Theme
- An inline script in `<head>` sets `.dark` from `localStorage` or `prefers-color-scheme` before first paint.
- A ThemeProvider syncs state and the toggle. Dark mode uses the blueprint's palette.

## 8. Layout

### 8.1 Desktop (≥1024px)
- Max width 1440px, outer padding 48px, gap 32px.
- Implemented as `lg:grid lg:grid-cols-3 lg:gap-8`, where each column is a `flex flex-col gap-8` stack.
- **Card heights use aspect ratios / min-heights, not the blueprint's fixed pixel heights.** Columns are balanced so their bottoms land within about 24px of each other at 1280 and 1440 widths. The builder tunes this.

```
Column 1                     Column 2                          Column 3
[Ask Introhive — tall,  ]   [About mini | Experience mini ]   [Theme toggle card   ]
[ device/diagram visual ]   [Hero: monogram, headline,    ]   [Plant as Battery    ]
[ Shipped chip          ]   [ status chip, buttons:       ]   [ In progress chip   ]
[Slang-or-Not           ]   [ Résumé · Copy email ·       ]   [Career ruler        ]
[ In progress chip      ]   [ GitHub · LinkedIn           ]   [Introhive MCP (sm)  ]
[Stack card             ]   [Résumé pill banner (inverted)]   [Fredericton clock   ]
                            [Personality card (PRs)       ]
                     Footer: LinkedIn/GitHub glass buttons + © 2026 Saran Chotsuwan
```

The arrangement may be adjusted by the builder to balance column heights, as long as Ask Introhive stays the tall top-left card and the hero stays top-centre.

### 8.2 Mobile (<1024px)
A single-column flow:
1. Header: monogram + theme toggle.
2. Hero (`min-h-[calc(100svh-120px)]`).
3. Projects.
4. About (bio, experience timeline, career ruler, clock).
5. Résumé and links.
6. Footer.

A fixed bottom glass dock has 4 items (Home, Projects, About, Contact), with IntersectionObserver scroll-spy. The progressive bottom blur is `pointer-events: none` and sits below the dock.

## 9. Components and behaviour

- **Hero:** CSS-animated SVG monogram "SC" (original design). Headline (draft example: "Software engineer building AI that actually ships."), sub-line, status chip and buttons. If per-word hover color cycling is kept, the headline element carries `aria-label` with the full sentence and the spans are `aria-hidden`. "Copy email" shows "Copied" and falls back to `mailto:` if the clipboard API is unavailable.
- **Glass system:** the blueprint's 4-layer glass button and chip, with `@property --glass-angle-1/2`. **Always pair `backdrop-filter` with `-webkit-backdrop-filter`, and `mask-composite: exclude` with `-webkit-mask-composite: xor`.** Provide a solid-fill fallback via `@supports not (backdrop-filter: blur(1px))`.
- **Accent color:** an original accent distinct from the reference's Android green, chosen by the builder and easy to change via one token. Status colors: Shipped = green-ish dot, In progress = blue dot (generic status semantics, not branding).
- **Project cards:** `data-cursor-text` is not needed. Cards are real `<a href="/projects/slug">` links, intercepted for overlay navigation, so they work with middle-click, crawlers and no-JS. Videos, if any, use `autoPlay loop muted playsInline` plus a `poster`, and are paused when off-screen or when reduced motion is set.
- **Placeholder visuals:** per-project SVG illustrations, e.g. a RAG pipeline diagram for Ask Introhive, MCP client→server→tools for Introhive MCP, Pi→lights→forecast loop for Plant as Battery, and a token-highlight sentence pair ("the soup is **salty**" / "she was **salty** after losing") for Slang-or-Not. They're in the site's style, not grey boxes.
- **Case study overlay:** full-screen, scrollable, with focus trapped, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, Esc to close, and body scroll locked. Top-right sticky theme toggle + close. Hero media up to 1100px, text column up to 860px. A metadata strip (Product/Org, My role, Timeline, Stack, Team). Section blocks rendered from `sections[]`. Prev/Next glass buttons at the bottom.
- **About overlay:** bio, experience timeline and skills, from content files.
- **Experience mini card:** the three roles as a compact list. Clicking opens About.
- **Career ruler:** ticks span Saran's timeline in years. Scrubbing shows the year and the role at that time, with a "Reset to present" button. Keyboard operable (arrow keys) with an ARIA slider role.
- **Clock card:** `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with `timeZone: 'America/Moncton'` for both the analog and digital displays. The second hand ticks per second under reduced motion and sweeps otherwise. Label: "Fredericton, NB".
- **Personality card:** powerlifting PRs (squat/bench/deadlift/total) as big numbers. Content comes from `personal.ts`.
- **Résumé:** a PDF in `public/`, linked from the hero button and the banner, opening in a new tab. The current PDF is a placeholder, and swapping it is one file replace. The PDF includes Saran's phone number. Saran decided to keep it in the web copy for now (2026-11-26).
- **Photos:** About mini card, About overlay and mobile About show a circular photo, with the SC monogram as the fallback.

## 10. Quality bar

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: disables Lenis, in-view animations, the overlay slide (replaced with a fade) and the hover color cycling.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in both themes, visible focus rings, semantic headings, and alt text from content files. Lighthouse Accessibility ≥ 95.
- Performance: Lighthouse Performance ≥ 90 on mobile for `/`. Fonts are preloaded `woff2` with `font-display: swap`, images are AVIF/WebP with explicit dimensions, and nothing above the fold is lazy-loaded.
- SEO: per-route titles, descriptions and OG tags (Section 7.3), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`.
- **Verification before calling it done:**
  - Playwright screenshots in Chromium **and WebKit** at 390×844, 768×1024, 1024×768, 1280×800 and 1440×900, in light and dark.
  - axe checks with zero serious or critical violations.
  - Lighthouse on `/` and one case study.
  - Direct-load and Back/Forward tests for every route.
  - `tsc` and `eslint` clean.
